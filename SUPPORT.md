# Support

Thanks for using one of our open source libraries!

## Bug reports and feature requests

Please open an issue on the repository's GitHub issue tracker. Include your
Delphi version, target platform and, for bugs, a minimal reproduction. We
actively maintain our libraries and review issues and pull requests regularly.

## Questions

Questions are welcome as GitHub issues too. For anything that does not fit an
issue, reach us through [gdksoftware.com/contact-us](https://gdksoftware.com/contact-us).

## Commercial support

Our libraries are MIT licensed and free to use. For companies that depend on
them commercially we offer:

- **Support and maintenance agreements** with guaranteed response times and
  priority handling of your issues
- **Sponsored development**: we build and maintain the features your product
  needs, in the open, as part of the library

[GDK Software](https://gdksoftware.com) is a Delphi specialist: we build,
upgrade and maintain Delphi applications worldwide, and offer Delphi and AI
consultancy and AI training. Contact us at
[gdksoftware.com/contact-us](https://gdksoftware.com/contact-us) to discuss
what you need.
